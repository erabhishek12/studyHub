About StudyHub

Study Smarter, Not Harder ✨

StudyHub is a free, ad-free student productivity platform built by Abhishek (Er. Abhishek), a Full Stack Developer. It gives students access to notes, video lectures, previous year question papers, programming tutorials, 12+ study tools, and now — interactive quizzes to test their knowledge.
